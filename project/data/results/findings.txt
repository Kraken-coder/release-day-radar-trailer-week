Across 49 eligible films, surviving trailer-week comment volume has Spearman correlation 0.74 with opening-weekend India nett. On the 13 later-release test films, adding comments to the Hindi/regional baseline reduced MAE by 31.6%, from ₹21.79 crore to ₹14.89 crore. The sample and retrospective surviving-comment measure are too limited to establish a reliable general forecasting rule. RMSE changes from INR 29.91 crore to INR 24.32 crore, and leave-one-out errors differ from the forward holdout: the conclusion depends on the metric and evaluation split. The expanded Spearman 95% film-bootstrap interval is [0.52, 0.88], compared with 15 films and rho=0.78 in the original sample.

Recommendation: Use comment volume only as an experimental supporting forecast input, alongside local advance bookings, screens and comparable releases; do not allocate screens from it alone.
